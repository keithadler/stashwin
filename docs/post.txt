Stash for Windows. Free, open source.

Encrypted backup of the folders that matter into the storage you already have: the OneDrive that comes with 365, the Google Drive that comes with Gmail, Dropbox, iCloud, a disk, a NAS. Several at once, on a schedule, uploading only what changed.

Everything is encrypted on the PC first; the provider only ever holds scrambled blobs. Your key is 24 words on a card. No password, no recovery, no server: lose the card and nobody can read the backup, including you. Verify restores a random file to prove the whole path works.

Same format as Stash for Mac, byte for byte. One card restores on either.

No account, no update check, nothing leaves the PC unencrypted. One exe, x64 and ARM64, no installer, never an administrator. Also a command line: stash backup

Second Windows app from the maker of Quiet for Windows and Clip, Tidy, Stash, Permissions, Ask and Watch for Mac.
